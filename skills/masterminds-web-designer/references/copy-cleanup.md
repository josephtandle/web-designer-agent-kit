# Copy Cleanup Guide

Make copy cleaning a normal build step before visual QA.

Use the participant's explicit voice profile first, requested tone second, and existing voice third. Joe's voice is not a substitute for the participant's voice.

## Protect meaning and evidence

Rephrasing is allowed when the new wording preserves meaning, facts, evidence, and qualifications. Preserve exact quotations, technical and product names, dates, prices, legal language, citations, links, SEO fields, and wording the participant wants kept. Do not strengthen a qualified claim, turn an intention into a result, or invent proof.

Edit visible prose in a safe copy. Do not alter HTML structure, attributes, classes, scripts, CSS, destinations, or metadata as a side effect of copy cleanup.

## Review method

1. Mark good lines to keep before looking for problems.
2. Run the local checker when available, treating its output as review leads rather than a detector verdict or automatic rewrite.
3. Review inflation, empty contrasts, forced conclusions, synonym cycling, flat rhythm, clichés, and prompt residue in context.
4. Draft changes in a safe copy.
5. Show representative before/after examples with a short reason.
6. Compare protected facts, evidence, qualifications, exact quotations, and destinations.
7. Check wrapping in an observed mobile render when available.

Examples:

- Before: "We leverage cutting-edge solutions to transform your journey."
  After: "We help you move forward."
  Reason: removes inflated mechanics without inventing a more specific service or result.
- Before: "Clients can see up to 20% faster onboarding, depending on their current process."
  After: "Depending on the current process, clients may see up to 20% faster onboarding."
  Reason: improves rhythm while retaining the qualification and numeric claim.
- Keep: "ISO 27001", a product name, an exact client quotation, a stated price, or a legal disclaimer.

Remove em dashes from newly rewritten prose unless they occur inside an exact quotation. The checker warns only. It does not establish authorship, rewrite copy, or guarantee quality.
